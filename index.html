ℹ️ About ViewEarn

How points work

You earn points by watching videos in the app and by spinning the daily lucky wheel (one free spin per day). Points convert to money at a fixed rate, shown on the Videos tab (currently 100 points = $0.10).

How withdrawals work

Once you have at least 5,000 points ($5), you can request a withdrawal from the Wallet tab. Requests are reviewed and paid out to the payment reference you provide; you can track the status of each request in your Withdrawal History.

Referrals

Share your referral link from the Games tab. When a friend signs up with your link and completes offers or surveys, you earn a 10% bonus on their offer earnings. Referral bonuses are paid on real completed offers only, not on sign-ups.

Fair use

Accounts found using automated tools, multiple fake accounts, or other abuse to generate points may be suspended and forfeit their balance.

❓ Help & Support

Why can't I earn points yet?

You need to verify your email first. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for the verification link, then return to the Videos tab and tap "I've Verified - Recheck".

Why is my spin button disabled?

The lucky wheel allows one spin per day. It resets at midnight UTC.

How long do withdrawals take?

Withdrawal requests are reviewed manually and typically processed within a few business days. You'll see the status change from "pending" to "paid" in your Withdrawal History.

My withdrawal was rejected — why?

This usually means the payment reference you provided couldn't be verified. Double-check the email, phone number, or account details and submit a new request.
